Grand9K_Pixel_Cyrillic.ttf is a derivative of the above, made for this fork and used for all maptext
(runechat, balloon alerts, signboards). It is Grand9K Pixel with the Russian alphabet added; every glyph
the original had is untouched. The Cyrillic glyphs are taken from Grand9K_Pixel_modif.ttf as distributed
by ss220-space/tgstation (https://github.com/ss220-space/tgstation/tree/master/interface/fonts), itself a
CC BY 4.0 adaptation of Jayvee Enaguas' Grand9K Pixel. Rebuild and verify with tools/fonts/.
