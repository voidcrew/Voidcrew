Server Rules

Know the zones. Ignorance is not an excuse.

Zone Rules

Green Zone

No combat or stealing. No griefing at all.

Yellow Zone

Piracy and stealing are allowed. Escalation is required for murder — if someone steals your stuff or attacks you, they're free game.

Red Zone

Kill on sight.

Outposts

  • No griefing the outpost itself.
  • Otherwise, combat follows the zone rules.

Metagaming

  • Discord / metacomming is totally fine.
  • Don't metagame — no multikeying, no using ghost to scope something out.

Conduct

  • No slurs, and don't be excessively shitty.
  • NO ERP.
Rules are enforced at admin discretion. When in doubt, ahelp.
