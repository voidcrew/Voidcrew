An orbital drop pod is a reinforced closet that fires from your ship down onto the planet you are sitting over. It exists so a crew does not have to land the whole hull to put people on the ground — the ship stays in orbit and keeps flying, while whoever is in the pod goes down to a spot of their choosing. It is a one-way trip: each pod fires once.

## Getting one

Drop pods are crafted, not bought. The recipe is in the equipment section of the crafting menu:

| Pod | Materials | Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Orbital drop pod | 30 iron sheets, 5 rods | Opens itself when it lands |
| Advanced orbital drop pod | 15 plasteel, 15 iron, 10 rods | Tougher, insulated against outside heat and pressure, and stays sealed on landing |

The advanced pod is what you want for a hostile world: it holds its own air and temperature while you decide whether to open the door. Goon hulls have a pod aboard from the start.

## Launching

Click the pod to bring up its control panel. Use it to open the doors, get in, and close them again — it will refuse to launch with the doors open. Then pick one of two launches:

- **Random launch.** Free, always available. Drops you on a random clear spot on the surface, never inside a cave.
- **Directed launch.** Puts you behind a targeting camera over the planet and lets you pick the tile. It needs the pod linked to a research server with a multitool, and that server has to have the advanced survey console research. Further tiers of that same research add object and then creature sight to the targeting view, so you can see what you are about to land on.

The targeting view marks each tile green or red. It refuses walls and unmined rock, chasms and anything else with no floor, the cordoned-off ground past the planet's edge, and any tile with a megafauna standing on it. Everything else is fair game — a pod crushes what is under it.

Requirements, in short:

- The ship has to be sitting on a planet's tile, and that planet's surface has to be live. A planet that has been abandoned long enough to release its surface — see [Planets](planets.md) — will not take a pod until a ship docks there and brings it back up.
- The pod has to be aboard the ship, closed, and unused.

## Getting home

The pod is a one-shot. It stays where it lands and makes a serviceable shelter, but it will not fly again.

Plan the trip back before you launch:

- **Link a quantum pad.** Buffer a quantum pad with a multitool and use the multitool on the pod before launching. Once the pod is on the ground, its Teleport button sends everything inside it — or, if the doors are open, everything on its tile — to that pad. It works exactly once, and the pad has to be powered and intact when you press it.
- **Or have the ship collect you.** The alternative is the ship landing at the planet's berth and the pod crew walking to it. That costs nothing but time, and it is the usual answer when the whole crew went down together.

!!! warning "One pod, one trip"
    A pod that has fired cannot be re-armed and the teleport cannot be used twice. Two people who both want a ride home need either two pods or a plan.

See [Planets](planets.md) for what is waiting on the surface, and [Piloting](piloting.md) for getting the ship into orbit over one.
