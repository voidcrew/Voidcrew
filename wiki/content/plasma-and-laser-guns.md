Four frontier guns turn up at traders and in loot. The Kestrel and Hornet are ordinary laser guns that charge in a recharger. The Cinder pistol and Furnace rifle fire bolts of burning plasma, and you feed them by hand with plasma sheets or ore. See [Weapons & Gear](weapons-and-gear.md) for the rest of the armoury.

## The guns

| Gun | Kind | Ammunition | Shots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel laser pistol | Light laser pistol, fits a belt | Recharger | 20 |
| Hornet laser SMG | Fully automatic laser, worn on the back | Recharger | 20 |
| Cinder plasma pistol | Plasma sidearm, fits a belt | Plasma flask | 15 a flask |
| Furnace plasma rifle | Heavy plasma rifle, worn on the back, needs both hands | Plasma flask | 12 a flask |

The Kestrel hits lighter than a standard laser gun. The Hornet fires the standard laser beam for as long as you hold the trigger.

The **Old Furnace** is a Furnace with a hand-wound coil. It runs cooler and shoots straighter. Nobody sells it.

Each line restores one unit per hour. Guns bought at a counter come with a full flask seated. See [Trader Outposts](trader-outposts.md) and [Crews & Reputation](zone-reputation.md).

In loot, Kestrels turn up in green armory and freight caches, Hornets and Cinders in yellow caches, and Furnaces in red plunder, where an Old Furnace is a rare find. A plasma gun found in loot carries a flask that is at most half full. Pirates in yellow and red zones fire plasma guns, and in red zones Director Prime and the Chief Auditor carry Furnaces and can drop the Old Furnace. They never drop it anywhere else. See [Pirates & NPC Ships](pirates-and-npc-ships.md).

Plasma and frontier guns cannot be crafted, printed or ordered through cargo, and no one buys them back.

## Plasma flasks

A plasma gun fires from the flask seated behind its grip.

- **Load:** use a flask on the gun. Any flask already seated comes out first.
- **Unload:** Alt-click the gun to take the flask out. The plasma left in the gun goes back into the flask.
- **Fill:** use plasma sheets or plasma ore on a flask, or on the gun to fill the seated flask. One sheet or ore goes in at a time while you stand still. A full flask takes **3 sheets** or **6 ore**.
- **Check:** examine a flask to see roughly how full it is.

A flask only takes plasma sheets and ore. Liquid plasma, plasma gas and other flasks do nothing. Plasma never comes back out of a flask: it leaves only as shots or vented gas. Rechargers do not work on plasma guns, and an EMP does not drain them.

Plasma ore turns up in every zone, so a crew that mines can keep a plasma gun fed. One sheet holds as much as two ore.

## Plasma bolts

Plasma bolts are slower than lasers and bullets, so a target far away can step out of the way.

- **Armour:** bolts are energy hits. Ablative armour and reflective vests cannot reflect them.
- **Burns:** bolts leave deep burns that get worse with every hit on the same spot. They do not set people on fire. Treat them like any other burn.
- **Limbs:** an arm or leg that is already badly hurt melts away under a bolt. A rifle bolt also melts an arm or leg that already carries a critical burn. One bolt never melts a healthy limb. Heads and chests never melt.
- **Glass and doors:** bolts stop at windows and break them. They break windows, tables, other structures and doors faster than other guns: a shuttle window takes two rifle bolts or four pistol bolts, an airlock about four rifle bolts. Grilles and blast doors shrug them off, and mechs and machines take no extra damage.
- **Fuel gas:** a bolt passing through plasma, tritium or hydrogen in the air can light it. Clean air never lights.
- **Canisters:** bolts do not damage gas canisters.

Hits leave scorch marks on the floor that stay until they are cleaned. Scorch marks on a ship are gone after a ship save.

## Heat and venting (Furnace only)

The Furnace and the Old Furnace heat up as they fire, and their coils glow brighter as they do. They cool by themselves, fastest in cold air at normal pressure, slower in thin air, and slowest in vacuum. Standing in fire or on lava heats them up.

- A full flask fired as fast as you can in ship air will not overheat a Furnace. In vacuum it will.
- An overheated rifle **hisses and spits gas**: it dumps some plasma onto your tile and the gun takes damage. A rifle that keeps overheating eventually breaks.
- **Vent:** use the rifle in your hand to dump some plasma and cool it quickly. You can't fire for a moment afterwards, and you have to wait a few seconds between vents. A rifle with no plasma left has nothing to vent.

!!! warning "Vented gas is hot"
    Vented and spat plasma comes out hot, and hot plasma can catch fire in breathable air. Vent in vacuum when you can.

The Cinder does not heat up.
