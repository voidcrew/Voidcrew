Crews launch without lethal guns. Find weapons in guarded loot, buy limited trader stock or make them through ordinary crafting and research. Dangerous zones improve your chances of finding stronger equipment.

## Starting equipment

Ship lockers, crates and job kits do not supply lethal guns. Security and miners receive survival knives; disablers and work tools remain available. Hulls with MOD storage carry ordinary suits rather than command-grade equipment.

The first chaplain of a round receives a null rod. Its starting choices exclude guns and certain high-end melee forms. Surgery kits contain their operating tools; Sawbones sells one at the Patch-Up Clinic for **750 credits**.

## Buying weapons

Cargo does not sell security equipment, individual combat guns or conversion kits. Visit [trader outposts](trader-outposts.md). Their stock is shared with every crew.

| Gun | Counter | Price | Reputation required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surplus bolt-action rifle | Barnaby, Halcyon | 900 cr | None |
| Disabler | Sarge, Quartermain | 400 cr | None |
| Laser gun | Sarge, Quartermain | 1,500 cr | None |
| Riot shotgun | Sarge, Quartermain | 1,800 cr | 3 green points |
| Energy gun | Sarge, Quartermain | 2,400 cr | 3 green points |
| C-20r | Vex, Undertow | 3,600 cr + 2 vouchers | 3 green points |
| WT-550 | Vex, Undertow | 4,200 cr + 2 vouchers | 3 green points |
| Mateba, when stocked | Vex, Undertow | 3,000 cr + 1 voucher | 3 green points |

These gun lines restore one unit per hour up to their stock limit. Convoys do not refill them early. Most lethal lines start with one gun; Barnaby starts with two rifles. Limited showcase weapons may never restock. Check [zone reputation](zone-reputation.md) for access requirements.

Barnaby also sells hunting and survival knives. Sarge sells combat knives, shortswords, ablative bats, fire axes and sabres. A riot shield costs **1,200 credits**.

## Finding weapons

Loot weapons follow three broad groups:

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| Green | Surplus rifles, pistols, detective revolvers, double-barrelled shotguns; survival knives, spears, hatchets and bats |
| Yellow | Laser and energy guns, WT-550s, C-20rs, Uzis and Tommy guns; combat knives, shortswords, fire axes and sabres |
| Red | Carbines, laser cannons, L6 SAWs, X-ray guns, sniper rifles, combat shotguns and Bulldogs; claymores, katanas and energy swords |

These are loot groups, not restrictions on what you may carry. Green loot can rarely roll stronger equipment. Yellow and red improve the odds.

A cache makes one main weapon roll. For an armory cache the gun chance is **12% in green, 32% in yellow and 55% in red**. Other themes have lower chances. A failed gun roll gives a melee weapon. The remaining contents follow the cache's theme.

Abandoned freight crates have an **8%, 19% or 33%** gun chance by zone and otherwise give melee. Their gun selection stops at the yellow group. Purchased mystery caches also use this restricted weapon selection.

NPC weapons and drops vary by zone. The weapon an enemy uses does not guarantee that it drops. Strong melee prizes such as the hierophant club and cleaving saw are no longer guaranteed boss rewards. Unguarded ruin floors and starting lockers do not provide the strongest weapons. See [Ruins](ruins.md) and [Hostile Fauna & Elites](threats-and-elites.md).

Pirate bounty weapon bundles hold one gun each. Other bundles can contain armour or security supplies instead.

## Crafting and research

Use the ordinary crafting menu for improvised guns, melee weapons and shields. Research unlocks printable weapons and conversion kits as usual. Fit each kit to the gun named by its recipe.

Kit research needs crew [zone reputation](zone-reputation.md):

| Research | Unlocks | Reputation required |
|---|---|---|
| Electric Weaponry | Tesla, ion and temperature kits | Yellow |
| Advanced Beam Weaponry | Advanced energy gun and X-ray kits | Advanced |
| Illegal Technology | Energy crossbow kit and rapid syringe gun | Elite |
| Unregulated Bluespace Research | **Event Horizon** kit | Elite |
| Illumination Applications | Photon cannon kit | None |

The requirement covers every design on each node. You still need its usual prerequisites and research points. Imported research remains usable.

The Event Horizon recharges itself and fires a long-range beam that opens a brief reality tear on impact. It has **no scope**. Keep it away from your own crew and hull.

## Ammunition and firing pins

Traders sell ammunition, and the [research tree](research.md) unlocks magazines and specialty loads. A 50-round L6 SAW magazine costs **50 iron, 10 titanium, 10 plasma and 5 plastic sheets** before lathe discounts. Its 80-point node requires Exotic Ammunition and Automatic Ammunition. Fully upgraded lathes reduce the material cost to 40%.

Pistol, revolver, shotgun shell and rifle-clip ammunition needs no reputation. Magazines for SMGs, rifles and the C-20r and WT-550 need yellow [zone reputation](zone-reputation.md) to research; L6, Bulldog, sniper and explosive ammunition need advanced.

**No Quarter** uses heavy cartridges, which fit no other gun. Anyone can craft one from **two iron sheets and one paper** in three seconds, with batch crafting available. Reloading takes five seconds. The gun arrives with twelve cartridges in its bandolier.

Sarge sells ordinary **firing pins for 250 credits**. Fit one to a gun that lacks a pin. Removing a fitted pin destroys it. **Riot Supression** research also unlocks the printable **Test-Range Firing Pin**, which only allows firing near a test range.

## Suits and rare prizes

Ruin suit storage chooses its stock from the zone where it appeared: ordinary MODsuits in green, security in yellow, and an unrestricted nuclear suit in red. Moving the storage unit does not improve its stock.

Wick sells suits, parts and modules at Quartermain. A civilian suit costs **1,800 credits**. Vex sells stronger chassis with reputation requirements. Trader anomaly-powered modules do not yield a removable anomaly core.

Elite and specialist suits, the captain's laser, the Meteor Piledriver and certain ship boards can also arrive as rare completion prizes for guarded [missions](missions.md). Check that page for eligible work and limits.
