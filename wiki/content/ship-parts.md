**Ship parts** are physical loot you can bank to your account and spend on permanent shipyard unlocks. Collect them during a round, put them in an extraction case, and carry the case when you extract. Banked parts stay with your account across characters and rounds. They are separate from the credits and trade vouchers covered in [Money & Trade](money-and-trade.md).

## The four classes

Every part belongs to one of four classes. Examine it to check its class before deciding what to keep.

| Class | Colour | Places to look |
|---|---|---|
| Combat | Red | Shipwrecks, armory and syndicate loot, pirate bounty rewards |
| Science | Blue | Research sites, laboratory, medical and occult loot |
| Trade | Gold | Old stations, trade posts, industrial and wardrobe loot |
| Misc | Grey | Derelicts, wrecks, expedition caches and general loot |

These are loot sources, not guarantees that every container holds a part. Purchases require the classes shown in their price. For example, the Goon's Chem Lab module costs **4 science parts**; combat, trade and misc parts cannot cover that cost.

## Finding parts

Explore [ruins](ruins.md) and open their loot containers. Different sites favour different classes, so choose destinations that can supply the parts you need. There are also rewards you can deliberately pursue:

| Source | Ship-part reward |
|---|---|
| [Pirate bounties](pirates-and-npc-ships.md) | Light bounties pay 1 part and heavy bounties pay 3. Each is combat or misc, with combat more likely. Collect the loose parts from the mission pad after turning in the bounty. |
| [Contested cache](dynamic-events.md) | A bonded prize crate with 5 parts and a standard extraction case. The parts favour combat but can be any class. |
| [Prospect Stake missions](missions.md) | Some hard contracts offer a bonded crate of ship-grade components instead of vouchers. Check the contract's reward text. The crate contains 5 parts and a standard extraction case. |
| [Grand Colosseum](colosseum.md) | The match prize pool scales with the roster and is divided among the winners in champion's extraction cases. Parts favour combat; see the Colosseum guide for prize sizes and claiming them. |

These rewards still need to be extracted. Parts arriving on a pad or in a prize crate have not reached your account yet. Move the parts from a bonded prize crate into its case, then pick up the case.

### The round-end participation part

If you played a character and are still connected at round end, you receive **1 part of a random class** directly into your account. This reward needs no extraction case and still pays if you died or already extracted. Staying in the lobby or observing without joining a character does not qualify.

## Getting an extraction case

Use **Request Extraction Case** in the **IC** tab for a free standard case, then pick it up. The verb refuses while you are already carrying an extraction case, including one inside a backpack.

A standard case holds **5 ship parts** and accepts only ship parts. Examine it to check how many are inside. Carry it in your hands or inventory; **inside a backpack counts**. A case left on the floor or in a ship locker does not bank its contents.

### Locking the case

Use the case in hand to open its keypad. Enter a five-digit PIN and press **E** to set it, then **C** to lock it. Enter the PIN and press **E** to unlock it again. Locking is optional for extraction.

The PIN protects access to the contents, but someone can still steal the whole case. A screwdriver opens a locked case's service panel, and a multitool can reset its PIN with about forty seconds of work. An emag permanently destroys the lock.

## Banking your parts

You can extract in either of two ways:

- **Bluespace jump:** carry the case aboard your ship when it jumps out of the system from the [helm](piloting.md#bluespace-jump).
- **Round end:** carry the case when the round ends. You do not have to be aboard a ship for this.

At extraction, you must be **alive, connected and still controlling your character**. Unconscious characters can extract; corpses, disconnected characters and characters you have ghosted out of cannot.

!!! warning "Check your case before leaving"
    Loose parts never extract, even if they are in your backpack. The parts must be inside a case you are carrying. A bluespace jump removes the ship and everyone aboard from the round, so agree with your crew before using it.

The **current carrier** receives the parts. The case is not bound to whoever requested it or set its PIN. You can carry a locked case and bank its contents without knowing the code.

Each account gets **one extraction per round**, using the first standard case found in its carrier's inventory. Carrying more standard cases does not increase that allowance. Consolidate the parts you want into one case before extracting. An empty case does not use your extraction, but extracting even one part does. Respawning does not reset the limit.

### Champion's extraction cases

A Colosseum champion's case holds up to **16 parts** and banks **alongside** your standard case. Carry both at the same extraction to bank both. Champion's cases do not grant a second extraction after you have already banked parts that round. Only their ship parts carry over; any prize credits or vouchers remain in-round rewards.

## Trading parts with another player

You can hand over parts you found, or use **Withdraw Ship Part** in the **IC** tab to turn a banked part back into a physical item. Choose a class from the menu; one part is deducted from your account and appears at your location.

Give the item to the recipient so they can put it in their case and extract it. They receive the account credit only when they extract, and their usual extraction limit still applies. A withdrawn part can be lost or stolen like any other physical part.

## Spending banked parts

Open the **SHIPYARD** tab on the lobby's dock terminal to see your balance and the prices of hulls, themes and modules. The bill for a ship adds up everything on it you don't own yet, and **COMMISSION** pays it from your banked parts in one go. You do not carry physical parts into the shipyard. Free options need no parts.

Unlocks are permanent. Buying a theme or module lets you select it for future launches of that hull without paying again, and losing a ship does not erase your purchases. See [Buying & Upgrading Ships](ship-upgrades.md) for choosing a loadout and [Ship Catalog](ship-catalog.md) for the available hulls.

[Saving a ship](player-outposts.md) in a ship bay also costs parts. The hull costs misc parts, which can be paid for at 2,500 credits each. The rest pays for the progress a save carries, in matching classes: upgraded weapons cost combat parts, upgraded lab and chemistry machines and research (1 science part per 50 nodes) cost science parts, and upgraded cargo and mining machines cost trade parts. Machines at their standard parts are free, upgrades and research together cost at most 10 parts, and every save costs at least 5 parts in total. Funds carry at 1 trade part per 5,000 credits.

A ship's join password can be paid for with parts of any class. Setting or changing it costs **3 parts**; clearing it is free.
