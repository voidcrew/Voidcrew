Each [trader outpost](trader-outposts.md) has someone on the concourse who hands out work that is on no board: sabotage, smuggling, a safe, a killing, a jailbreak. The pay is good and the outpost's security is the price. Everything you do on a job is an ordinary crime if somebody sees it, and a job nobody hears about pays more.

## Finding work

| Outpost | Who | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Waystation Halcyon | Nan Tolliver, an old woman with a paperback and a thermos | A corner seat in the Chowder Pot |
| Quartermain Depot | Lot Brannigan, a man with a clipboard and a pen behind his ear | The coffee counter in the crew room |
| The Undertow | Mister Quill, a thin man in a dark suit, dealing himself cards | The end stool at the Dregs |

They are ordinary people: they can be hurt, cuffed and killed like any townsperson. A stand-in arrives 20 minutes after one dies, and jobs already taken stay good.

Click one with an empty hand. The menu offers **Got any work?**, **What else?**, **I'll take it.**, **It's done.** and **Forget it.** They turn you away, and say why in their own words, when:

- you have no ship;
- anyone on your crew is wanted at that outpost, or an officer is close and watching;
- the outpost is shut or still cleaning up after a killing;
- your crew already has a fixer job, on whichever ship;
- they have nothing for you yet;
- you hurt one of them where somebody saw it in the last 30 minutes. That holds with every fixer, for you and for any ship you crew, and re-forming the crew does not clear it.

Each fixer keeps two jobs and offers them one at a time. Only you read the offer; the room sees them lean in. It stays open for three minutes, and if another crew takes it first you are told it is gone.

You need the [reputation](zone-reputation.md) to work in the zone of the outpost where the job is done, as with any contract. Fixer jobs give no reputation.

## Taking a job

**I'll take it** starts the job and hands you a folded note with the person or thing, the place, the time and the pay. Losing or burning the note changes nothing, and anyone who picks it up can read it. Your ship's mission list shows the pay and the deadline. Deadlines run whether or not you are at the outpost.

Jobs are done at the fixer's own outpost, except smuggling, which runs to another one. The pay is for the zone the job is given in.

| Job | Neutral / Contested / Lawless | Deadline | Offered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sabotage | 800 / 1,360 / 2,080 cr | 20 min | from the start |
| Smuggling | 600 / 1,020 / 1,560 cr | 40 min | from the start |
| The safe | 1,200 / 2,040 / 3,120 cr | 25 min | after 2 jobs paid |
| A killing | 1,500 / 2,550 / 3,900 cr | 20 min | after 2 jobs paid |
| Evidence | 1,600 / 2,720 / 4,160 cr | 25 min | after 5 jobs paid |
| A jailbreak | 2,400 / 4,080 / 6,240 cr | 20 min | after 5 jobs paid |

Jobs paid counts every job a fixer has paid your crew for, quiet or loud. A failed job does not lower it.

## The jobs

**Sabotage.** Freight, a new drinks machine or a courier's data rack has been brought to the concourse, with someone minding it. Ruin every piece of it, by smashing it or tampering with it. It counts when the last piece is ruined. Ruining it is the *sabotage* crime: seen once it is a star, and it never goes past two.

**Smuggling.** You are handed a sealed case to take to the fixer at the outpost named on the note. You must be allowed to work in that zone. The checkpoint there refuses the case as it refuses a gun, and nobody is wanted for trying. The ways past are the gate's usual ones: storage the scanner cannot see into (found, never sold), an emag on the scanner, a pried door, or a dead guard and no witnesses. See [the checkpoint](trader-outposts.md#the-checkpoint). Handing it to that fixer is the deed and the payment in one step. The case cannot be destroyed or taken from you.

**The safe.** A courier firm's deposit safe has been brought to the concourse, with a ledger inside. The combination is on a slip in the pocket of a clerk who drinks. Lift it while he is asleep, out cold or dead, listen to the dial, or blow the safe. Opening the safe and taking the ledger out are theft if seen. It counts when the ledger is out in your crew's hands.

**A killing.** A visitor has come to the outpost. The note gives his name, his look and the time his shuttle leaves. Kill him before then and bring his papers to a fixer.

**Evidence.** A sealed evidence bag sits in the outpost's security locker, beside the desk sergeant. The locker opens for a security ID or an emag, and taking the bag out where it is seen is theft whether or not the lock was broken. It counts when the bag is out; the fixer pays for the bag and nothing else in the locker.

**A jailbreak.** A prisoner is in a cell at the outpost's security station. Force the door, which is always three stars, and lead him out. He follows you, and counts the moment he steps aboard your ship. If you go down or he loses you for half a minute, he gives up and the officers take him back. Once aboard he stays a while and then goes his own way. See [outpost security](trader-outposts.md#outpost-security) for cells.

## Getting paid

Tell any fixer **It's done**, with what the job asks for in your hand: the case, the ledger, the bag or the papers. The money goes to your ship's account and you read one line saying how much. A smuggled case has to go to the fixer on the note.

A fixer will not pay while anyone on your crew is wanted at their outpost or an officer is close. If reports of the job are still on their way, they ask you to wait a minute. Once the deed is done you have an hour to collect.

## Quiet pays more

If security never hears of the job, the fixer adds up to 60% to the price. The louder it gets, the smaller the addition, and at three stars or more there is none. A loud job still pays what the note says. What counts is the highest alert raised by your crew, by anyone who hurt the job's people or things, or by the port itself when you finished. A jailbreak is judged from three stars up, because a cell always sees its door forced.

Fixers rest between jobs. After a payment, a fixer has nothing for your crew for six minutes, or ten if security heard of the job. After a failed or dropped job, the fixer who gave it waits ten minutes.

## Your own work

The fixer pays for your crew's work. If someone outside your crew does the deed for you, by killing the mark, picking up the case, the ledger or the papers, or walking the prisoner out, the job is spoiled and pays nothing.

## When it goes wrong

A job fails when its deadline passes, when you choose **Forget it** or abandon it at the ship console, when its subject is lost, or when it is spoiled. Nothing is paid, and there is no fine or debt. Everything the job brought is gone with it.

Security answers a job like any other crime. The pay is highest at the Undertow, and from three stars up every officer there shoots to kill.
