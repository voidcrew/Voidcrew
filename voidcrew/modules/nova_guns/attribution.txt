The art and sounds in this module come from Nova Sector (https://github.com/NovaSector/NovaSector),
taken on 2026-11-08 from the paths below. Nova licenses its assets CC BY-SA 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) unless a file says otherwise, and none of these
files says otherwise; they are used here under that licence. Each DMI holds only the states these
guns use, copied pixel for pixel, apart from the changes listed.
