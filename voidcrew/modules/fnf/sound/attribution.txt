Friday Night Funkin': Corruption+, by Carlito049 (https://gamejolt.com/games/fnfcorruptionplus/793089),
a fan extension of the Corruption mod by Phantom Fear and Pincer. Used with credit, as Carlito asks.
Its characters: Kapi by PaperKitty, Skarlet Bunny by Rechi, Carol by bb-panzu. Broken Wires by FellowDesert51.
icons/fnf_notes_kapi.dmi, icons/fnf_notes_skarlet.dmi - its tainted arrows (NOTE_assetsKapi,
NOTE_assetsSkarlet, noteSplashesKapi), made small
icons/fnf_healthbar_tainted.dmi - Skarlet's health bar frame (hpBar)
icons/fnf_speakers.dmi - the speakers and the corrupted ones (speakers, EVILspeakers), made small
icons/fnf_props.dmi - Marble's speaker (from speakers), made small, and Kapi's dance pads, drawn after
his sprites (kapi, corruptedkapi)
icons/fnf_corruption_overlays.dmi - its overlays and flashes (scary, prey, hunter, shadowarcade,
overlayskarlet and the rest), at a quarter size
Its songs and their game overs (Kapi's, bloodlust and null) aren't shipped: tools/fnf/fetch_corruption_plus.py fetches them from GameJolt.
