Reagent transfer sounds (beakerpour_*.ogg, dropper*.ogg).

Ported from Monkestation2.0, which ships them under the GNU Affero General
Public License v3 (the same licence as this repository). Monkestation carries no
per-file attribution for this set; sound/chemistry/SoundSources.txt there covers
only the sounds in the parent directory, not these.

The "sparkle" variants are rare alternates, played roughly 1% of the time.

Note: beakerpour_10-25-4.ogg is included for completeness but is not referenced
in code, matching monkestation, whose 10-25 list skips it.
